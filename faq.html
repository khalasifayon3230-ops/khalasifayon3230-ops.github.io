❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Dream Hunt account? +
Go to the registration page, provide your required information and complete email verification. After verification you can log in to your Student Portal.
Where can I see my enrolled courses? +
After logging in, open Student Portal → Learning → Enrolled Courses.
How does course payment work? +
Select your desired course package from Buy Courses, complete the payment using the available payment method, then submit your transaction ID. Your payment remains pending until it is verified.
When will my course become available? +
Your course becomes available after your payment has been verified by Dream Hunt.
Can I edit my examination result? +
No. Students cannot edit examination results. Results are controlled and published by Dream Hunt administration.
Where can I find admission updates? +
Go to Student Portal → Admission → Admission Updates. There you can find available application dates, notices, admit cards, seat plans and result links.
Can I contact Dream Hunt directly? +
Yes. Use the Contact Us section from your Student Portal. You can contact Dream Hunt through WhatsApp, Telegram, phone or email.
Are the university links official? +
Always verify important admission information from the institution's official website before submitting an application or payment.
